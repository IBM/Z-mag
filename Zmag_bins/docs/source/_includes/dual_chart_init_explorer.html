Unit meridian: m = (0, my, √(1 − my2))

For this family on the unit sphere (x = 0, mz ≥ 0): m = (0, my, √(1 − my2)). Curves use the dynamic dual projection (forward maps plus exact-pole repair) and weights wA = ½(1 − m·P), wB = ½(1 + m·P) (Eq. 89). Pole P = (0, −1, 0), so m·P = −my.
